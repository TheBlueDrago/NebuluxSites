Questions

How long does it take?

Most websites are ready in 3 to 7 days after you pay. Bigger custom projects can take longer; we'll tell you before you pay.

Can I ask for changes?

Yes. We show you the site before it goes live and make changes until you're happy with it.

How do I pay?

First a $5 request fee, which comes off your final price. After we review your request, you pay the rest. Both links show on your account page with a Pay button.

Do I need to know how to code?

Not at all. Just tell us what you want in your own words.

Can I use my own domain (like mybakery.com)?

Yes. Tell us your domain in your request and we'll connect it for you.
