FAQ

Questions? Answers.

Still curious? Press Help and ask Nebula, our AI agent.

How long does it take?

It depends on your plan. One time ($199) is top priority: about 1–2 weeks. 3 months ($75/month): about 2–3 weeks. 5 months ($49/month) is standard priority: about 1 month. These are estimates, not guarantees.

How do I pay?

Sending a request is free. When we accept it, you pay a $5 starting fee on the Billing page (it comes off your price). When your website is finished, you pay the rest there. We email you each time.

What do I get at the end?

A ZIP file with your whole website: every page, picture and file. You upload it to your own hosting, and we give you clear, step-by-step instructions.

Do you buy the domain and hosting for me?

No. You buy your own domain (like mybakery.com) and hosting, for example from Cloudflare, Netlify, GoDaddy or Hostinger. Our price is for building the website, and we tell you exactly how to put it online.

Can my website take payments (like an online shop)?

Yes, we can build checkout and payment buttons into it. But you need to set up your own payment provider account (like Stripe, PayPal or Square), so the money goes straight to you. We give you the steps to connect it. Any fees are set by that provider.

What do I need to set up myself?

Three things, and we show you how: your domain name (like mybakery.com), your hosting (where the website lives), and a payment provider account if your website sells things or takes payments. These aren't included in our price.

Can I ask for changes?

Yes. You can watch the live preview the whole time, and we make changes until you're happy with the design you described.

Do I need to know how to code?

Not at all. Just tell us what you want in your own words.
