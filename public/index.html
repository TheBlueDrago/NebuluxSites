Questions

How long does it take?

It depends on your plan. One time ($199) is top priority: about 1–2 weeks. 3 months ($75/month): about 2–3 weeks. 5 months ($49/month) is standard priority: about 1 month. These are estimates, not guarantees.

Can I ask for changes?

Yes. We show you the site before it goes live and make changes until you're happy with it.

How do I pay?

Sending a request is free. When we accept it, you pay $5 (it comes off your price). When your website is finished, you pay the rest. We email you each time, and the Pay buttons are on your account page.

Do I need to know how to code?

Not at all. Just tell us what you want in your own words.

Can I use my own domain (like mybakery.com)?

Yes. Tell us your domain in your request and we'll connect it for you.
