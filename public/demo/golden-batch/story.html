It started with one tray.

Golden Batch began in a home kitchen with a single tray of chocolate chip cookies for the neighbors. They kept coming back, so we kept baking.

Today we still mix every batch by hand, brown our own butter and bake before sunrise, so the first box out the door is always warm.
